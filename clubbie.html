The problem

Running a club meant five tabs and a clipboard.

As AAPI president I tracked members in one spreadsheet, meeting sign-ins in Google Forms, service hours in another sheet, and events in a group chat. Every report at the end of the year was a weekend of copy-paste. Clubbie pulls all of it into one place.

How it works

From join code to year-end report.

Start a club

Sign up, create your club, and get a join code plus a /join link to share.

Members join

Members sign up and enter the code or tap the link. Nobody adds anyone by hand.

Make a meeting

Every meeting gets its own QR code with a check-in window that opens and closes on its own.

Scan to check in

Project the QR. Members scan from their phones and show up on the list live.

Log service hours

Members submit hours with proof. Officers approve, and everyone's totals update automatically.

Print the report

Analytics and a printable end-of-year report come straight from the real data.

QR attendance

Check-in takes about four seconds.

Each meeting has a unique token. Duplicate check-ins are blocked, attendance shows up live, and officers can still fix any record by hand.

Everything else

The parts clubs actually use.

Members

A searchable roster with each member's attendance and hours history. Promote, deactivate or fix records in a click.

Service hours

Submit with optional proof, then an approval queue. Rejected entries stay in the history.

Ranks

Create custom ranks with their own name and color, put them in order, and choose which ones get officer or admin access.

Events & RSVPs

Capacity limits, going / can't-go, and a live count of spots left.

Analytics

Attendance over time, membership growth and top members, drawn with hand-built SVG charts.

Club report

A print-ready end-of-year summary. It's the thing advisors and schools ask for.

Under the hood

Small stack. Strict rules.

Next.js 15Server ActionsTypeScriptSQLiteTailwindzod
  • The server checks every actionSession, club membership and role (owner, officer or member) are re-verified on the server for every action. The browser is never trusted.
  • Zero-config storageSQLite in WAL mode keeps it fast and easy to deploy. Nothing to provision.
  • Private by defaultOnly name, email and school are collected. Proof uploads are stored privately, and club data is visible only to that club's members.
  • Built for phones and laptopsMembers check in and log hours from their phones with a bottom nav. Officers run the club from a full desktop sidebar.
