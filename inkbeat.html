The problem

Notes app on one side, YouTube scrubbing on the other.

When you write to a beat you spend half your time dragging the scrubber back to the hook and trying to remember which bar went where. Voice memos pile up with names like "new recording 47." Inkbeat ties every lyric and every take to the exact second it belongs to.

  1. Paste a beatDrop in a YouTube link. The song takes the video's title.
  2. Drop markersHit M while it plays, wherever a verse or hook lands.
  3. Write per sectionEach marker gets its own lyric page, timestamped.
  4. Record a flowThe mic starts and the beat plays from that marker with a lead-in.

Covers & parodies

Import a song. Rewrite it line by line.

Search any song and Inkbeat pulls in its timed lyrics, already split into sections. Write your own words under each original line, then swap in a karaoke track and record your version over it.

  • Timed importEvery original line lands on the second it's sung.
  • Side by sideShow or hide the original while you write.
  • Karaoke swapOne click finds the instrumental; playback picks up at the same spot.

Recording

Takes live where they were written.

01

Record flow

Start recording on any marker. The mic opens and the beat plays from that spot.

02

Keep every take

Record as many takes as you want per marker. Rename them, download them, delete the bad ones.

03

Play it back over the beat

Hear any take mixed over the beat from the same second, or switch the beat off to hear just your voice.

04

Lead-in

Get 2–8 seconds of beat before the marker so you come in on time.

05

Sync

Nudge takes earlier or later in milliseconds when Bluetooth lag throws the timing off.

06

Backups

Everything saves in the browser on its own. Export the whole notebook as one file whenever you want.
